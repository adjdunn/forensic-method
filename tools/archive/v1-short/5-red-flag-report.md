# 5. Red-flag report

Run last, in the same conversation as prompts 1 to 4. Then paste this prompt.

---

Using everything found earlier in this conversation, write a one-page forensic review of the company.

**Rank the items to investigate by strength of evidence, strongest first:**
1. A sentence contradicted by the company's own numbers.
2. Two or more signals pointing at the same account in the same period.
3. A single measure out of line with the company's history.

**Structure:**
- **Header:** the company, the periods covered, and the documents used.
- **Top five items to investigate.** For each:
  - what the filings show, with page references;
  - the innocent reading;
  - the concerning reading;
  - what to read next to settle it.
- **Checked and explained:** items that looked unusual but have an ordinary explanation in the filings, such as an acquisition or a new accounting standard.
- **Not examined:** what the documents did not allow.
- **Closing line:** "Items to investigate, not conclusions. The verdicts are the analyst's."

**Rules:**
- Use only what is in the attached documents and the earlier answers in this conversation. Add no new numbers.
- State nothing more strongly than the evidence behind it.
- Don't use words like fraud, manipulation or red flag in your own sentences.
- Keep it to one page, or a page and a half at most.
