# 4. Consistency check

Use the same conversation, with the financial statements and annual reports attached. Then paste this prompt.

---

Test whether the company's sentences agree with its numbers.

**Run each check that applies:**
1. **Impairment.** A statement that there are "no indicators" or "no triggering events", against the unit's results. Is there a decline over several periods, or a sale or review of the unit mentioned elsewhere?
2. **Useful lives and holding periods.** Stated lives, holding periods or residual values, against the depreciation rate (depreciation ÷ average gross equipment) and any ranges disclosed. Look for a stated average that stays the same while its range widens or the rate falls.
3. **Explanations.** Sentences that explain a movement ("due to", "as a result of", "reflecting") against the figures they rely on, including other sentences in the same note.
4. **"Non-recurring" items.** Items described as non-recurring or one-time, against whether they appear in other years.
5. **Reserve releases.** A release described as a "change in estimate", against the reserve rollforward: did payments or claims actually fall?
6. **Prior-year figures.** Last year's figures as reprinted this year, against the figures as first reported. Any difference the company does not explain.

**For each check, record:**
- the sentence, quoted exactly, with the document and page;
- the number it was tested against, calculated in code;
- the result: **consistent**, **inconsistent**, or **can't test** (say what's missing).

An inconsistency needs both halves to be in the attached documents. Don't rely on anything you know about later events.

**Rules:**
- Use only the attached documents. No outside knowledge of this company.
- Do every calculation in code. Don't show the code unless asked.
- For every inconsistency, give the innocent explanation first, then the concerning one.
- These are things to check, not findings. Don't use words like fraud, manipulation or red flag.

**Reply in about one page:**
1. A table: Check | Sentence (quoted, page) | Number | Result | Innocent reading | Concerning reading.
2. One line summarizing what was inconsistent.
