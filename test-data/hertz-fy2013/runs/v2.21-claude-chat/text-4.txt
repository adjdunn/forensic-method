Hertz Global Holdings, Inc.: disclosure changes, FY2012 to FY2013
Compares the Form 10-K reports for the years ended December 31, 2012 and 2013 and the two fourth-quarter results releases.
"Pre-tax" is FY2013 income before income taxes of $663.1M.
1. Full write-ups
Depreciation policy (Note 2): car holding range lengthened and relabelled
 Before: "Useful lives are as follows: Cars | 4 to 28 months" (FY2012 report, p. 81).
 After: "Holding periods are as follows: Cars | 4 to 36 months" (FY2013 report, p. 91).
 Why it matters: The top of the range rose by eight months, and the company names "optimization of fleet holding periods"
among the reasons U.S. rate changes cut 2013 depreciation by $44.2M (p. 126). Neither report discloses the range
change as a change in estimate, and the holding-period share of the $44.2M is not split out. Size: 6.7% of FY2013 pre-tax
income of $663.1M.
 Reading: Possibly the reporting is stretched, because cars were depreciated over a longer span in the year U.S. disposals
swung to a $48.2M loss. Evidence that cars were in fact sold older, with disposal results back near break-even, would point
to an ordinary integration of the Dollar Thrifty fleet.
Depreciation (Note 8 and management discussion): "strong" dropped and the reason for the rate cuts
changed
 Before: "indicative of the strong residual values experienced in the U.S." (FY2012 report, p. 113); lower per-vehicle
depreciation was "due to improved residual values in the U.S." (p. 42).
 After: "also indicative of the residual values experienced in the U.S."; the cuts now "reflect changes from the impact of car
sales channel diversification… and the optimization of fleet holding periods" (FY2013 report, p. 126).
 Why it matters: The sentence tested in the single-year assessment is a carry-over with one word removed, while the U.S.
disposal result it rests on went from a $100.6M gain to a $48.2M loss. The management discussion also dropped "residual
values have remained fairly stronger" and the warning that a car's market value may "be less than its estimated residual
value" (FY2012 p. 41–42); the warning survives only in risk factors (FY2013 p. 23). Size: 6.7% of pre-tax (the $44.2M cut);
the disposal swing of $148.8M is 22.4%.
 Reading: Most likely the business weakened and the wording was trimmed to match, with the estimate itself left
favourable. A different reading would follow if the 2013 losses came mainly from one-off sales timing, which the note also
cites.
Controls and Note 2: prior years revised; Dollar Thrifty no longer outside the controls test
 Before: No error correction; "We have excluded from our evaluation the internal controls… of Dollar Thrifty", 21% of total
assets (FY2012 report, p. 141).
 After: New section "Correction of Errors… out of period errors totaling $46.3 million" (FY2013 report, p. 83); new general
ledger "implemented" in the third quarter (p. 159); the exclusion is dropped.
 Why it matters: 2012 pre-tax income is now $441.4M, not $450.6M, so company-wide pre-tax growth reads 50.2% rather
than 47.2%, and adjusted pre-tax growth 29.2% rather than 27.9%. Size: $34.7M moved to prior years, 5.2% of pre-tax;
$46.3M in total, 7.0%.
 Reading: Possibly the control conclusion is generous, as before; the comparison adds that the named errors (vendor
incentives, Brazil) are not attributed to the newly tested Dollar Thrifty. One isolated process, already fixed, would point to
ordinary.
2. Other changes
 Legal: The Nevada fee case moved from "a stay order and… mediation" (FY2012 p. 122) to restitution granted in part and
a judgment that "could potentially exceed $40.0 million" (FY2013 p. 137), an exposure of 6.0% of pre-tax. A securities
class action is new (p. 138).
 Pricing measure: "Rental rate revenue per transaction day", which left out items "beyond the control of management"
(FY2012 p. 49), became "Total RPD", which covers what management "has the ability to control" (FY2013 p. 49). The
2012 U.S. decline is 3.1% on the old basis (FY2012 p. 42) and 2.8% on the new (FY2013 p. 58).
 Adjusted pre-tax income: "non-cash debt charges" became "debt-related charges" (FY2012 p. 50; FY2013 p. 50).
Integration ($40.0M), relocation ($7.8M) and impairment ($44.0M) are new exclusions, together 13.8% of pre-tax. The
releases raise the "normalized" tax rate from 34% to 35% (2012 release p. 20; 2013 release, unnumbered page), against a
reported 47.8%.
