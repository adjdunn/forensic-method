Cost line: direct operating plus depreciation of revenue earning equipment and lease charges. Substitutes: other property at
cost includes land; the M-score uses total liabilities ÷ total assets for leverage and cash, receivables, inventories and
prepaid expenses as current assets; both Q4 income statements come from the FY2013 results release (the spreadsheet's
Q4 2013 is mixed basis); rental depreciation is the Note 8 charge before the disposal adjustment, FY2011–13 as shown in
the FY2013 report. Industry: Transportation averages (rental and leasing has no row of its own).
Flags, largest first
1.  Receivables fell 19.8% in FY2013 while revenue rose 19.4%, taking days sales outstanding from 76.5 to 51.3 (Q4: 74.8 to
 54.4) after two years of increases. Vehicle-sale proceeds sitting in receivables fell from $617.3m to $357.3m, and the
end-2012 balance included Dollar Thrifty, acquired November 2012 (2012 acquisitions outflow $1,904.6m).
Size: 113% of pre-tax income ($746m: receivables at FY2012 days, $2,258.9m, less the $1,512.6m balance). It has run
one year.
2.  Payables are 9.0% of sales against 4.5% for the industry, which is the Transportation group, not rental and leasing.
 Payables include amounts owed for fleet purchases ($289.1m sat in payables and accrued liabilities at end-2013).
Size: 73% of pre-tax income ($483m: the $967.9m balance less 4.5% of sales). The share exceeds 4.5% at all four
year-ends, though the industry figure is for 2013 only.
3.  Payables fell 3.1% in FY2013 while costs rose 19.2%, taking days payable from 52.7 to 42.7 (Q4: 50.9 to 42.8). The
 end-2012 balance included Dollar Thrifty's payables against about six weeks of its costs.
Size: 34% of pre-tax income ($226m: payables at FY2012 days, $1,194.2m, less the $967.9m balance). It has run one
year, and two Q4 comparisons (52.1, 50.9, 42.8).
4.  The depreciation rate on rental equipment fell from 16.1% (FY2011) to 15.2% to 14.3%. Note 8 reports rate reductions
 on U.S. rental cars that cut the charge by $139.4m in 2012 and $44.2m in 2013, and the adjustment on disposal swung
from a $96.8m gain to a $37.2m loss.
Size: 23% of pre-tax income ($152m: the charge at the FY2012 rate on average gross rental equipment of $16,878.9m,
less the $2,407.8m taken). It has run two years.
5.  Q4 gross margin fell from 22.1% to 18.5% while the full-year margin held (23.0% to 23.2%). Direct operating costs rose
 from 54.2% to 56.3% of revenue, and depreciation and lease charges from 23.7% to 25.2%.
Size: 14% of pre-tax income ($90m: the 3.5-point fall times Q4 revenue of $2,556.3m; the quarter's revenue is used, not
the year's, because the fall is one quarter's). It has run one Q4 comparison.
Revised figures: the FY2013 annual report lowered pre-tax income by $18.7m for FY2011 (324.3 to 305.6) and $9.1m for
FY2012 (450.5 to 441.4), net income by $11.9m and $4.5m, and end-2012 equity by $21.1m (2,507.3 to 2,486.2); the
fiscal-year columns above are as first reported.
Not calculated: Q4 accruals and free cash flow (the releases give no quarterly operating cash flow and the spreadsheet's
Q4 2013 cash flow is mixed basis); quarterly depreciation rates (the note is annual); FY2010 growth gaps, accruals,
depreciation rates and M-score (no 2009 balance sheet); land and construction in progress (not shown separately); industry
gross margin.
