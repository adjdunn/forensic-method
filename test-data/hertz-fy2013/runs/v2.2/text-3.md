**1. Full write-ups** (all pages are in the FY2013 annual report)

**Depreciation rates cut while U.S. cars sold at a loss** (Note 8, p. 126)
- **The note says:** the rate cuts were "indicative of the residual values experienced in the U.S." in all three years, yet 2013 disposal losses came from "declining residual values from falling demand for used vehicles" (both p. 126).
- **Test:** Holds for 2012 and 2011, not 2013: rate changes cut U.S. car rental depreciation by $44.2M (6.7% of company pre-tax income) while U.S. disposals swung from a $100.6M gain to a $48.2M loss.
- **Reading:** Most likely the reporting stretched: 2013 rates look low for what U.S. cars actually fetched, which defers cost. A quick return to disposal gains would point to ordinary timing.
- **Read next:** Rate changes and disposal results by quarter in the 2013 10-Qs and the first quarter of 2014.

**Four years of errors corrected, controls still called effective** (Note 2, p. 83)
- **The note says:** "out of period errors totaling $46.3 million" were not material to earlier years, but correcting them at once "would have been material to the fourth quarter" (p. 83).
- **Test:** Holds as arithmetic: $46.3M is 74.3% of fourth-quarter pre-tax income of $62.3M. The earlier-years claim is closer: 2011 pre-tax income falls 5.8% and diluted EPS 7.5%; 2012 pre-tax income falls 2.0%.
- **Reading:** Possibly controls weaker than reported: the errors ran from 2010 into 2013, yet management calls controls "effective as of December 31, 2013" (p. 159). Their small size against annual income would point to ordinary clean-up.
- **Read next:** The controls section of the next 10-Q, and any further revision.

**Recurring charges labelled one-time** (Note 11, p. 131)
- **The note says:** adjusted pre-tax income leaves out "certain one-time charges and non-operational items" (p. 131).
- **Test:** Does not hold for restructuring: excluded every year ($77.0M, $38.0M, $56.4M) and $645.4M since 2007 (p. 140). All exclusions total $490.1M, 73.9% of 2013 company pre-tax income.
- **Reading:** Most likely the reporting stretched: a cost booked seven years running is treated as outside normal operations. The label would fit if restructuring ends with the Dollar Thrifty integration and the headquarters move.
- **Read next:** Whether the further relocation spend "in the range of $40 million to $45 million" (p. 65) is excluded too.
