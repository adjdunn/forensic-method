Lines used (annual sheets for years, quarterly sheets for Q4; all values as originally reported):
- **No cost-of-sales line.** Days inventory and days payable use *Direct operating* (IS). Gross margin = *Total revenues* − *Direct operating* − *Depreciation of revenue earning equipment and lease charges*; operating margin also deducts *Selling, general and administrative* (IS).
- Working capital: *Receivables, less allowance*, *Inventories*, *Accounts payable* (BS). Allowance: the receivables caption on the balance sheet in each annual report ($20.3, $25.1, $30.8).
- Accruals and free cash flow: *Net income*, *Net cash provided by operating activities* (CF), *Total assets* (BS). Free cash flow deducts fleet and property spending net of disposal proceeds.
- Fleet: *Revenue earning equipment expenditures* less *Proceeds from disposal of revenue earning equipment*, *Depreciation of revenue earning equipment* (CF), over average *Cars* + *Other equipment* at cost (BS). Property: the matching *Property and equipment* lines (CF) over *Total property and equipment, at cost* (BS).

1. **Receivables fell while revenue rose.** FY2013 *Receivables* −19.8% (1,886.6 to 1,512.6, BS annual) against *Total revenues* +19.4% (IS annual); days sales outstanding 51.3, against 65.5 to 76.5 in FY2010 to FY2012. *Sales of revenue earning equipment included in receivables* fell 617.3 to 357.3 (CF annual). The FY2012 year-end balance follows 1,904.6 of acquisitions.
2. **Prior years changed in the FY2013 annual report.** *Income before income taxes* for FY2011 324.3 to 305.6 (−5.8%) and FY2012 450.5 to 441.4 (−2.0%); *Total equity* at December 2012 2,507.3 to 2,486.2 (Revisions sheet).
3. **Fourth-quarter margin.** Q4 FY2013 gross margin 18.1% against 22.2% (Q4 FY2012) and 21.3% (Q4 FY2011): *Direct operating* +17.5% on *Total revenues* +10.0%; *Provision for taxes on income* 58.6 on *Income before income taxes* of 50.7 (IS quarterly). Q4 is derived by subtraction from the annual figures.
4. **Fleet depreciation rate drifting down as the fleet grows.** 15.3% (FY2011), 14.6%, 14.5%; Q4 annualised 12.9% against 13.8%. Net fleet spending rose from 0.89x to 1.24x depreciation (CF annual, BS annual: *Cars*, *Other equipment*). FY2012's rate is diluted by fleet acquired late in the year.
5. **Other assets and payables against activity, FY2013.** *Prepaid expenses and other assets* +52.6% (470.1 to 717.2) and *Restricted cash* +50.4% (571.6 to 859.9); *Accounts payable* −3.1% against *Direct operating* +19.9%, days payable 61.4 from 76.2 (BS annual, IS annual).
