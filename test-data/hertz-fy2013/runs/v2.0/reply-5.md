**Hertz Global Holdings, Inc.: where to spend reading time**

Fiscal years 2009 to 2013 and the fourth quarters of 2012 and 2013. Documents: the statements workbook (as first reported, with its Revisions sheet), key sections of the FY2012 and FY2013 annual reports, and both fourth-quarter results releases. USD millions.

**Top five items to investigate**

**1. Fleet depreciation was cut again while cars sold at a loss** (a sentence against the company's own numbers)
- **Shown:** Note 8 calls lower rates "also indicative of the residual values experienced in the U.S." (FY2013 annual report, p. 126) and, in the same note, blames sale losses on "declining residual values" (FY2013 annual report, p. 126). Rate changes cut U.S. depreciation by 44.2 in 2013 and 139.4 in 2012 (31.6% of that year's pre-tax income); U.S. disposals swung from a 100.6 gain to a 48.2 loss. The word strong was dropped (FY2012, p. 113) and the longest holding period for cars went from 28 to 36 months (pp. 81, 91).
- **Most likely reading:** the reporting stretched, in its estimates, on top of a weakening used-car market.
- **Would change it:** evidence that the 2013 cuts covered only cars sold at or above book value, or that rates were raised late in the year.
- **Next:** rate changes and gains or losses per car by quarter; residual-value assumptions; the effect of the longer holding period.

**2. Controls were called effective in a year of errors, mismatched totals and a missed financing test** (a sentence against other sentences)
- **Shown:** errors of 46.3 over 2010 to 2013 in three unrelated areas (FY2013, pp. 83 to 89), but 47.9 in the release a day earlier (Table 1). The doubtful-accounts provision is 45.9 in the cash flow statement and 41.9 in Schedule II (pp. 80, 158). An earlier year's tax reserve changed from 21.6 to 41.4 without comment (FY2012, p. 116; FY2013, p. 129). A financing requirement was "unknowingly not met" (FY2013 annual report, p. 108). A possible judgment above 40.0 is called immaterial (p. 137) while the 46.3 was material to a quarter. Controls: effective (p. 159).
- **Most likely reading:** can't tell from these documents whether these are isolated slips during a ledger change or something wider.
- **Would change it:** each difference reconciled, or further corrections in later filings.
- **Next:** the correction tables by quarter in Note 2; how each error was found and what was re-tested.

**3. The fourth quarter of 2013 weakened on several fronts at once** (several signals, one period)
- **Shown:** gross margin 18.5% against 22.1% on the revised basis; U.S. fleet up 22.4% against rental days up 16.1%; tax of 62.9 on pre-tax income of 62.3 (FY2013 release, p. 2, Tables 1, 4, 5). Guaranteed airport fees rose 53.5% while revenue-based fees fell 6.8% (FY2013, p. 129). Corporate EBITDA ended 7.5% below the low end of guidance. The company's explanation, weaker pricing and excess fleet, held when tested.
- **Most likely reading:** the business weakening.
- **Would change it:** evidence that the quarter absorbed costs belonging to earlier quarters, which leads back to item 2.
- **Next:** fleet size against rental days in the next two quarters; a quarterly tax reconciliation.

**4. Receivables and payables both fell while revenue rose 19.4%** (several signals, one period)
- **Shown:** receivables down 367.1 on the revised basis, 261.3 of it unpaid fleet sales; the rest fell 8.4%; write-offs rose 37.5% (FY2013, pp. 75, 81, 133, 158). Payables without fleet and property purchases fell 13.6% while direct operating costs rose 19.7%; 3.1 of under-accruals was already corrected (pp. 75, 81, 87).
- **Most likely reading:** receivables, mainly ordinary (timing of vehicle sales, itself a sign of slower disposals); payables, can't tell.
- **Would change it:** a breakdown showing trade receivables or accrued costs out of line with volumes.
- **Next:** receivables by type with an ageing; payables and accrued liabilities by type; cut-off around the new payables system.

**5. Adjusted earnings leave out a growing list, including the Advantage loss, and pay depends on them** (several signals; wording changed)
- **Shown:** adjusted pre-tax income exceeded reported by 490.1 (74%); excluded lines rose from 7 to 11; assumed tax is 35% against an actual 47.8% (FY2013 release, p. 5, Table 6; FY2013, pp. 131, 134). The 44.0 charge on cars leased to Advantage's failed buyer is excluded while 65.0 of sublease revenue stays in (pp. 101, 146). Share awards vest on Corporate EBITDA (p. 125).
- **Most likely reading:** ordinary in form, since each item is disclosed; can't tell how much recurs.
- **Would change it:** exclusions shrinking in 2014 with no new labels.
- **Next:** amounts owed by Simply Wheelz and whether sublease revenue was collected; award outcomes in the proxy statement.

**Checked and explained**
- Beneish M-score below −1.78 in all three years (−2.92, −2.73, −3.25).
- The largest revised figures are movements between lines (new segments; accrued salaries folded into accrued liabilities).
- The error pieces sum to 46.3, and the two reports' depreciation-rate figures reconcile (139.4 less 8.8 is 130.6).
- The China Auto Rental notes (151.0) explain two-thirds of the rise in other assets; the self-insurance reserve held near 3.3% of pro forma revenue; property useful lives fit the depreciation charged.

**Not examined**
Management's discussion and critical accounting estimates (one page of Item 7 in each file); most risk factors; breakdowns of receivables, payables and accrued liabilities; claims paid against the self-insurance reserve; quarterly filings and the proxy statement; anything after March 2014.

Items to investigate, not conclusions. The verdicts are the analyst's.
