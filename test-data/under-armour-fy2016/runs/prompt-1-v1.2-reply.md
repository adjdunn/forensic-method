**2. Basis**
- Revised figures: the spreadsheet is as originally reported. Its Revisions sheet lists no change to net revenues, cost of goods sold, receivables, inventories or payables (the changes are to cash, prepaids, other assets, debt and per-share data). Q4 income statement figures are derived by subtraction.
- Acquisitions: two fitness-app businesses were bought in Q1 2015. Their "Other assets acquired" were $16.2m and $3.7m (FY2015 annual report, p. 55), against 2015 increases of $153.8m in receivables and $246.3m in inventory. CF annual, "Purchase of businesses, net of cash acquired", is 0 for FY2016.
