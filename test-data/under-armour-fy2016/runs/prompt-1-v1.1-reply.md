## Under Armour: receivables, inventory and payables, FY2013 to FY2016

**Basis.** The attachment is a workbook of the face financial statements only (USD millions, "as originally reported: each value comes from the first filing in the window that reported it", About sheet). Cost line used: "Cost of goods sold", so no substitute was needed. Days use actual calendar days (366 for FY2016, 91 for Q1 2016). Quarterly days use the quarter's own revenue or cost of goods sold. Gaps are in percentage points, year over year.

FY13 gaps are n/a because the workbook has no FY2012 balance sheet. Days sales outstanding is on total "Accounts receivable, net"; the workbook does not split customer from non-customer receivables.

**Revisions.** Every figure above is on the as-originally-reported basis. The Revisions sheet lists later changes to cash, prepaid expenses, other long term assets, long term debt, financing cash flows, and per-share and share-count figures. None of its rows touch receivables, inventories, accounts payable, net revenues or cost of goods sold, so these measures are the same on either basis.

**Acquisitions.** The cash flow statement shows "Purchase of businesses, net of cash acquired" of 539.5 in FY2015 (539.1 of it in Q1 2015) and 148.1 in FY2013; goodwill rose from 123.3 to 595.5 in Q1 2015. In the acquisition quarter itself the receivables and inventory gaps were negative (-6.0 and -3.0), and the Q4 2016 comparisons have the acquired business in both periods. What was bought, and what balances came with it, is not in the workbook.

### 3. Not calculated

The allowance for doubtful accounts as a percentage of gross receivables, the split of customer from non-customer receivables, and the acquired balances were not calculated, because the About sheet states that "Notes (allowance, inventory detail, useful lives) are in the annual report text, not here"; growth gaps for FY2013 and for 2014 quarters also need balance sheets earlier than the workbook provides.
