**Cost line:** Cost of sales, as reported. Substitutes: depreciation and gross property (less land and construction in progress) come from the annual reports' property note, and the M-score's accruals input is (net earnings less operating cash flow) ÷ year-end assets.

**Flags**

1. **Receivables outgrew sales.** "Receivables, net" (BS annual, BS quarterly) rose 47.3% in FY Feb-25 against "Net sales" up 4.5%, 14.2% against 3.2% in FY Feb-26 and 18.5% against 5.7% in Q2 Aug-26; days sales outstanding went from 7.9 to 12.4. Acquisition payments of 17,644 and 5,410 in those two years could explain it.
2. **Cash flow fell more than earnings in FY Feb-26.** "Net cash provided by operating activities" (CF annual) fell 17.6%, from 19,810 to 16,325, against net earnings down 4.4%; "Changes in accounts payable and accrued expenses" swung from 518 to -1,058 and "Changes in income taxes payable" from 809 to -698.
3. **Goodwill and intangibles are almost a third of assets.** "Goodwill" plus "Intangible assets, net" (BS annual) went from 12,061 (15.8% of total assets) at Jan-24 to 32,673 (31.1%) at Feb-26, which with receivables lifted the M-score to -2.12 in FY Feb-25, still below -1.78. The acquisition payments could explain it.
4. **Operating margin has fallen every year since FY Jan-23.** "Operating income" (IS annual) went from 15.3% to 12.7% of sales while gross margin moved only from 33.5% to 33.3%; "Selling, general and administrative" rose from 16.7% to 18.6% of sales.
5. **Inventory up, payables down in FY Feb-26.** "Merchandise inventories" (BS annual) rose 10.1% against cost of sales up 3.4% (days inventory 81.9 to 85.6) while "Accounts payable" fell 3.7% (days payable 41.7 to 38.1). The 5,410 acquisition payment could explain part of the inventory rise.
