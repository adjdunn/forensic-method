**Flags**

2. Inventory outgrew cost of sales: +11.8% against +4.4% in FY Feb-25, +10.1% against +3.4% in FY Feb-26; days inventory 75.1 to 85.6. GMS (acquired September 4, 2025) could explain part of the latest year.
   Size: excess balance at the prior year's inventory-to-cost-of-sales ratio, $1,568m, 8.4% of pre-tax income. Persistence: 2 consecutive years, plus a positive gap in both Q2 comparisons.

3. Operating cash flow fell 17.6% in FY Feb-26 against a 4.4% fall in net earnings: free cash flow to net income 1.19x to 0.89x over two years, accruals -7.9% to -2.2% of average assets. The annual report cites "timing of vendor payments and increased inventories" and a deferred tax payment (p. 37).
   Size: net earnings less free cash flow, $1,510m, 8.1% of pre-tax income. Persistence: 2 consecutive years of rising accruals and falling cash conversion.

4. Operating margin fell three years running, 15.3% to 12.7%, with gross margin flat and SG&A up from 16.7% to 18.6% of sales; still above the industry's 11.9%.
   Size: operating income forgone at the prior year's margin, $1,334m, 7.2% of pre-tax income. Persistence: 3 consecutive years.

5. Payables sit below the industry and fell in the latest year: 7.0% of sales against the industry's 9.4% (26% below); days payable 41.7 to 38.1 as payables fell 3.7% while cost of sales rose 3.4%.
   Size: shortfall against the prior year's payables-to-cost-of-sales ratio, $853m, 4.6% of pre-tax income (part of flag 3's cash gap). Persistence: 1 year (the prior year rose); negative in both Q2 comparisons.
