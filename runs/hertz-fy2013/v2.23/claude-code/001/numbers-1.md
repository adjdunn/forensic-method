**Flags**

1. **Operating margin far above the industry average:** 13.7% against 5.9% in FY2013 (2.3×). Hertz's margin is before interest, and the industry figure is a pre-tax operating margin.
   Size: $837m (126% of pre-tax income), taken as the margin gap × FY2013 revenue. Above the industry figure in all four years.
2. **Receivables fell far behind revenue:** days sales outstanding went from 76.5 to 51.3 (FY2012→FY2013) and from 74.8 to 54.4 (Q4 to Q4). Fleet-sale receivables in the cash-flow supplement fell from $618.6m to $357.3m.
   Size: $746m (113%), the balance at the prior year's days less the actual balance. One year falling, after two years of rises.
3. **Payables fell behind costs:** days payable went from 52.7 to 42.7 (FY2013) and from 50.9 to 42.8 (Q4). Payables are still 9.0% of sales against the industry's 4.5%.
   Size: $226m (34%), the balance at the prior year's days less the actual balance. One year falling; two same-quarter comparisons falling (Q4 2011 was 52.1 days).
4. **Rental equipment depreciation rate fell:** from 16.2% to 15.3% to 14.3% (FY2011–FY2013), and from 13.7% to 13.4% in Q4. The Dollar Thrifty acquisition in November 2012 ($1,904.6m cash) put a full fleet on the year-end 2012 balance with about two months of its depreciation, which lowers the FY2012 rate.
   Size: $175m (26%), the FY2013 charge not taken at the FY2012 rate. Two consecutive years falling.
5. **Q4 gross margin fell:** from 22.1% to 18.5% (Q4 2012→Q4 2013), while the full-year margin held at 23.2%.
   Size: $90m (14%), the fall × Q4 2013 revenue. One same-quarter comparison (Q4 2012 was up on Q4 2011).
