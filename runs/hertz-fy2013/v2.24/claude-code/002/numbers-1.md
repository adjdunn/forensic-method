Q4 2013 is marked mixed basis in the spreadsheet, so I took both Q4 columns from the FY2013 results release; their balances come from the spreadsheet. The quarterly accruals figure is not annualised. Other substitutes:
- **Depreciation:** "depreciation of revenue earning equipment" from Note 8. It is used for the depreciation rate, capex against depreciation and DEPI.
- **Capital spending:** rental equipment purchases and property and equipment purchases, each net of disposal proceeds.
- **M-score inputs:** the balance sheet is unclassified, so current assets are cash, restricted cash, receivables, inventories and prepaids. Total liabilities stand in for debt in LVGI.

**Flags**

1. **Receivables against the industry:** 14.0% of sales against 11.5% for the industry in FY2013. This is despite days sales outstanding falling from 76.5 to 51.3 (receivables −19.8%, revenue +19.4%). The FY2012 peak came with acquisitions of $1,904.6m, mainly Dollar Thrifty in November 2012.
   Size: $273.8m above the industry share of sales, 41% of pre-tax income ($663.1m). Persistence: days fell for 1 year after rising for 2.
2. **Payables fell behind costs:** days payable went from 52.7 to 42.7 in FY2013. Payables fell 3.1% while cost of sales rose 19.2%. The Q4 gap is −18.3 points. Payables are still 9.0% of sales against 4.5% for the industry.
   Size: $226.3m (payables at the prior year's days less the actual balance), 34% of pre-tax income. Persistence: 1 year, and Q4 moved the same way.
3. **Rental equipment depreciation rate fell:** 16.2% → 15.3% → 14.2% (FY2011–FY2013). Note 8 discloses residual-value rate changes that cut depreciation by $44.2m in 2013 and $139.4m in 2012.
   Size: $174.5m of charge not taken at the FY2012 rate, 26% of pre-tax income. Persistence: 2 consecutive years.
4. **Q4 gross margin fell:** 22.1% → 18.5% (Q4 2012 to Q4 2013).
   Size: $90.2m (3.5 points × Q4 2013 revenue of $2,556.3m), 14% of pre-tax income. Persistence: 1 comparison (Q4 2012 had risen from 21.3% in Q4 2011).

   Size: $31.7m (net income less free cash flow), 4.8% of pre-tax income. Persistence: 2 consecutive years.
