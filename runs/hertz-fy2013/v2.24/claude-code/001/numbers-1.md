**Notes on the table:**
- **Q4 source:** The Q4 2013 column in the spreadsheet is marked "mixed basis", so both Q4 income statements and operating cash flows come from the FY2013 results release. That puts Q4 2012 on the revised basis. Balances are the spreadsheet's period-ends.
- **Industry:** The industry figures are Transportation, the nearest bucket. The industry operating margin is its pre-tax unadjusted operating margin.

Cost line: direct operating plus depreciation of revenue earning equipment and lease charges. Substitutes:
- **M-score current assets:** cash, restricted cash, receivables, inventories and prepaids, because the balance sheet has no current/non-current split.
- **M-score leverage:** total liabilities.
- **Depreciation:** the depreciation of revenue earning equipment from Note 8, plus depreciation of property and equipment.

**Flags**

1. **Payables are about twice the industry's share of sales.** In FY2013 they were 9.0% of sales against 4.5%; the share has narrowed from 12.6% in FY2010. Payables and accrued liabilities include $289.1m of fleet purchases not yet paid.
   Size: 73% of pre-tax income ($483.2m: payables less what they would be at the industry share). Above the industry share in all 4 years.
2. **Receivables are above the industry's share of sales.** In FY2013 they were 14.0% against 11.5%, after falling from 20.9%. Receivables include $357.3m of fleet sales not yet collected.
   Size: 41% of pre-tax income ($273.8m: receivables less what they would be at the industry share). Above in all 4 years.
3. **Payables fell while costs rose.** Days payable fell from 52.7 to 42.7 in FY2013, and from 50.8 to 42.8 for Q4. Payables fell 3% while cost of sales rose about 19%. Dollar Thrifty was acquired on 19 Nov 2012, so year-end 2012 balances carry its payables against only about 43 days of its costs.
   Size: 34% of pre-tax income ($226.3m: payables at FY2012 days less actual payables). Ran for 1 year; for 2 consecutive same-quarter comparisons.
4. **The depreciation rate on rental equipment fell.** It went from 16.2% to 15.3% to 14.3% between FY2011 and FY2013. Note 8 reports changes to car depreciation rates for residual values, which cut the charge by $130.6m in 2012 and by $44.2m in U.S. car rental in 2013.
   Size: 26% of pre-tax income ($174.5m: the charge not taken at the FY2012 rate). Ran for 2 consecutive years.
5. **Q4 gross margin fell.** It dropped 3.5 points, from 22.1% to 18.5%, in Q4 2013 against Q4 2012. Note 8 shows FY2013 net losses on vehicle disposals, where FY2012 had gains (disposal adjustment +$37.2m against −$96.8m).
   Size: 14% of pre-tax income ($90.2m: the fall times Q4 2013 revenue). Ran for 1 same-quarter comparison (Q4 2012 was up on Q4 2011).
