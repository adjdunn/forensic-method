## 1. Write-ups

All page numbers are in the 10-Ks unless a results release is named. Pre-tax income (PTI) for 2013 is $663.1m.

**Revenue earning equipment (Notes 2 and 8): longer car holding-period range and new reasons for cutting depreciation rates**
- **Before:** "Useful lives are as follows: Cars | 4 to 28 months" (FY2012, p.81). The 2012 rate cuts were explained only as "indicative of the strong residual values experienced" (FY2012, p.113).
- **After:** "Holding periods are as follows: Cars | 4 to 36 months" (FY2013, p.91). The rate cuts are now explained by "car sales channel diversification, acceleration of our retail sales expansion and the optimization of fleet holding periods related to the integration of Dollar Thrifty" (p.126). Two sentences were also added to the critical estimates: "industry residual value guidebooks" and "Depreciation rates are adjusted prospectively through the remaining expected life" (p.45).
- **Why it matters:** The longest car holding period went up 29% (28 to 36 months) in the same year depreciation rates were cut by $44.2m. U.S. depreciation per car-month before disposal results fell 14.1%, from $241.6 to $207.7. The report does not present the longer range as a change in estimate, and gives no effect for it. Size: stated effect of the rate cut $44.2m = 6.7% of PTI ($39.6m = 6.0% net company-wide); the longer range itself is not quantified.
- **Reading:** Possibly the reporting stretched: holding assumptions were lengthened while the report's own U.S. sales results turned to losses. A different reading would be supported if the 28-month top of the range only reflected Dollar Thrifty's longer-held fleet, or if 2014 sales of these cars produce gains.

**Note 8: "strong" dropped from residual values while U.S. sale results turned to losses**
- **Before:** "indicative of the strong residual values experienced in the U.S." (FY2012, p.113). MD&A: "We believe the residual values have remained fairly stronger" (FY2012, p.42).
- **After:** "also indicative of the residual values experienced in the U.S. for the years ended December 31, 2013…" (p.126). The MD&A sentence is dropped, and MD&A now cites "a deterioration in the used vehicle residual values" (p.54).
- **Why it matters:** U.S. vehicle sales went from a $100.6m gain to a $48.2m loss, a $148.8m swing equal to 22.4% of PTI. The report removed "strong" but kept the claim that residual values supported the rate cuts and extended it to 2013. Last reply's test showed the claim does not hold for 2013. Size: the loss is $48.2m = 7.3% of PTI.
- **Reading:** Most likely the business weakening, with the wording trimmed instead of withdrawn. A different reading would be supported if the 2013 losses came only from the timing of sales, which Note 8 also cites, and residual values recovered.

**Legal (Note 13): first dollar figure on the Sobel case, and a new "not material" statement**
- **Before:** The settlement was rejected and "the parties will again be engaging in mediation". No amount and no conclusion on materiality (FY2012, p.122).
- **After:** "A judgment - which could potentially exceed $40.0 million" and "We continue to believe the outcome of this case will not be material" (p.137). This follows a partial summary judgment for the plaintiffs on restitution, plus class certification. A securities class action was also added (p.138).
- **Why it matters:** The first amount given equals 6.0% of PTI. The FY2012 report contains no earlier view on this case for the company to "continue" holding. Size: exposure ≥$40.0m = 6.0% of PTI.
- **Reading:** Can't tell. No accrual is disclosed. A different reading would be supported if an accrual existed or the March 2014 mediation settled well below $40m.

## 2. Other changes
- **Adjusted pre-tax income has new exclusions in 2013:** integration expenses $40.0m, relocation $7.8m, and impairment charges and other $44.0m. Together $91.8m = 13.8% of PTI. Wording changed from "non-cash debt charges" (FY2012 p.50; 2012 release p.19) to "debt-related charges" (FY2013 p.50; 2013 release definitions).
- **Cash-flow measures replaced:** the 2012 release's "Levered after-tax cash flow before fleet growth" (−$1,005.4m) and "Corporate cash flow" (−$2,183.4m) for 2012 (2012 release p.16) are gone. The 2013 release introduces "Free cash flow", which shows 2012 at +$155.1m after it "excludes certain DTG acquisition related items of approximately $129.6 million" (2013 release, Table 7).
- **Prior-year figures shown differently (error corrections):**
  - 2012 PTI: $450.6m → $441.4m (−2.0%).
  - 2012 adjusted PTI: $901.5m → $892.3m.
  - 2012 doubtful-accounts allowance: $25.1m → $29.3m, with the charge to expense $34.1m → $38.3m (FY2012 p.140; FY2013 p.84, p.158).
- **Multiemployer pension risk factor softened:** "Several of our remaining multiemployer plans have underfunded liabilities" (FY2012 p.25) became "Our multiemployer plans could have significant underfunded liabilities" (FY2013 p.26). Four plans are in "Critical" status in both years (FY2012 p.108; FY2013 p.121).
- **Controls:** a new sentence says the company "implemented Oracle general ledger, accounts payable and a portion of fixed assets" in Q3 2013 (p.159; not in FY2012 p.141). The $46.3m of out-of-period errors were found in Q4 of the same year. The audit opinion is unchanged apart from dropping the Dollar Thrifty carve-out.
