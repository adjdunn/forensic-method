## 2. Write-ups

**Adjusted pre-tax income: recurring restructuring presented as one-time**
- **The report says:** adjusted pre-tax income excludes "certain one-time charges and non-operational items" (Note 11, p.131). It is the audited segment measure.
- **Test:** The claim is that the excluded restructuring charges are one-time. Note 14 shows restructuring charges in every year presented: $56.4m in 2011, $38.0m in 2012 and $77.0m in 2013. It also says "we incurred $645.4 million" between 2007 and 2013 (about $92m a year) and "Additional efficiency and cost saving initiatives are being developed" (p.140). **The claim does not hold.** Size: restructuring plus restructuring-related charges excluded in 2013 come to $98.8m = 14.9% of PTI.
- **Reading:** Most likely the reporting is stretched. A cost that has appeared every year for seven years is part of running the business, so adjusted income overstates recurring earnings. A different reading would be supported if 2013's charges turn out to be tied to discrete events that end: the Dollar Thrifty integration and the $21.9m headquarters move.
- **Read next:** the 2014 restructuring charges, and the credit-agreement definition of "non-recurring" used for Corporate EBITDA (release, Table 7).

**Vehicle depreciation: rates cut while vehicles sold at a loss**
- **The report says:** "The cumulative effect of the reduction in rates was also indicative of the residual values experienced in the U.S." (Note 8, p.126).
- **Test:** The claim is that U.S. residual values realized in 2013 supported the $44.2m cut in depreciation. The matching figure is the 2013 result on U.S. vehicle sales. The same note gives "net losses of $48.2 million", attributed to "declining residual values from falling demand for used vehicles". MD&A also cites "a deterioration in the used vehicle residual values" (p.54). **The claim does not hold for 2013.** Size: the rate cut is $44.2m = 6.7% of PTI. The loss is $48.2m = 7.3% of PTI, but only 1.1% of U.S. disposal proceeds.
- **Reading:** Most likely the business weakening: used-car prices fell faster than the rate estimates, which the note says carry over "from previous quarters". The policy itself still leans aggressive, because the report cut rates in a year its own figures show losses on sale. A different reading would be supported if rates were raised within 2013 and the net cut came from changes made in 2012.
- **Read next:** the quarter-by-quarter rate changes and disposal results in the 2013 and 2014 quarterly reports.

**Goodwill: reporting units described two different ways**
- **The report says:** goodwill is tested "at the reporting unit level which is one level below our operating segments" (Note 2, p.93).
- **Test:** The claim is about the level of the reporting units. MD&A lists five reporting units: U.S. Car Rental, Europe, Other International, Donlen and Worldwide Equipment Rental (p.46). Note 11 names exactly these as the operating segments (p.130–131). So the units are the operating segments, not one level below them. **The claim does not hold.** Size: not quantified; goodwill is a $1,347.5m balance.
- **Reading:** Most likely ordinary wording left over from the Q3 2013 segment change. A different reading would be supported if units were combined, which would make an impairment less likely to show; no headroom is disclosed to check this.
- **Read next:** the reporting units and fair-value headroom by unit before the segment change.

## 3. Also noted
- **Tie-out gaps:**
  - Impairment is $40.0m in the cash flow statement and Note 15, but $44.0m as "impairment charges and other" in the adjusted reconciliation (p.80, p.146, p.134).
  - The doubtful-accounts provision is $45.9m in the cash flow statement but $41.9m in Schedule II (p.80, p.158).
- **Error corrections (p.83–85):** the sums hold ($6.8m + $18.7m + $9.2m = $34.7m for prior years; $11.6m for 2013). The 2011 correction is 5.8% of that year's as-reported PTI but is called not material. The main error was vendor incentives booked against marketing expense instead of vehicle cost.
- **Possible double count:** "Integration expenses" of $21.9m in other reconciling items (release, Table 6) equals the $21.9m headquarters relocation recorded within restructuring (p.140). Check that the same cost is not excluded twice.
- **Financing covenant:** a requirement under the HVF II Series 2013-B fleet notes was "unknowingly not met" at year-end and was waived in January 2014 (p.108).
