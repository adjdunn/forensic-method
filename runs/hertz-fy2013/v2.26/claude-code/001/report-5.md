**Hertz Global Holdings: FY2013 annual report (year to 31 December 2013), compared with FY2012**
Documents used: 10-K FY2013 and FY2012, Q4 results releases FY2013 and FY2012, statements.xlsx. Earlier replies are cited as text-1 and text-2 (the two report readings) and num-1 and num-2 (the two numbers replies). All sizes are shares of 2013 pre-tax income of $663.1m.

### Items to investigate

**1. Car depreciation: rates cut while the same note shows losses on car sales**
- **Shown:**
  - Note 8 says the U.S. rate cuts ($44.2m) were "indicative of the residual values experienced". The same note shows a $48.2m U.S. loss on sales "due to … declining residual values" (p.126), and MD&A cites "a deterioration in the used vehicle residual values" (p.54).
  - The word "strong" was dropped from that Note 8 sentence (FY2012 p.113).
  - The car holding-period range was extended from 4–28 to 4–36 months and relabelled from "useful lives" to "holding periods" (FY2012 p.81 → p.91). It is not presented as a change in estimate.
  - The U.S. average holding period stays at "eighteen months" (p.11, both years). Yet MD&A newly cites "longer holding periods" (p.53) and keeps "longer holding period does not necessarily equate to higher costs" (p.42).
  - The company's stated reason for the cuts: "car sales channel diversification, acceleration of our retail sales expansion and the optimization of fleet holding periods related to the integration of Dollar Thrifty" (p.126).
  - The fleet depreciation rate fell from 15.17% to 14.27% (revised basis).
  - Sizes (both are income effects):
    - Depreciation not charged at the 2012 rate: $152.1m = 22.9% (first reported $174.5m = 26.3%).
    - Company-stated rate cut: $44.2m = 6.7% ($39.6m = 6.0% net).
    - Loss on sales: $48.2m = 7.3%.
  - **Ranks on 22.9%.** Evidence level 1, in the audited notes.
  - Absorbs: text-1 profile row 3 and write-up 2; text-2 write-ups 1–2, table rows 1–5 and disclosed-changes rows 1–2; num-1 flag 5; num-2 item 2 and its estimate-change section.
- **Reading:** The replies disagree, and that is worth the analyst's time. Text-1 and text-2 write-up 2 read it as most likely the business weakening. Text-2 write-up 1 and num-2 read it as possibly the reporting stretched. Gains on later sales of 2013 cars, or better prices through the retail channel, would favour the ordinary reading.
- **Next:** quarter-by-quarter rate changes and sale results in the 2013 quarterly reports; how the 18-month average is computed; sale results by channel.

**2. Adjusted pre-tax income: "one-time" charges that recur every year**
- **Shown:**
  - Note 11 (p.131) defines the measure as excluding "certain one-time charges".
  - Note 14 shows restructuring in every year presented: $645.4m since 2007, with "Additional efficiency and cost saving initiatives are being developed" (p.140).
  - Restructuring plus related charges: $98.8m in 2013, $49.1m in 2012, $66.2m in 2011.
  - New exclusions in 2013: integration $40.0m, relocation $7.8m, impairment and other $44.0m, together 13.8%. "Non-cash debt charges" was renamed "debt-related charges".
  - Size: excluded from adjusted income, $98.8m = 14.9% (the amount the contradicted sentence covers). The total gap between adjusted and reported pre-tax income is $490.1m = 73.9%. Evidence level 1, in the audited notes; ranks after item 1 because an exclusion is not an income effect.
  - Absorbs: text-1 profile row 9 and write-up 1; text-2 other change 1; num-2 adjusted-earnings section.
- **Reading:** Most likely the reporting stretched (text-1). Charges that fall away once the Dollar Thrifty integration and the headquarters move end would point to ordinary.
- **Next:** 2014 restructuring charges; the credit-agreement definition of "non-recurring" behind Corporate EBITDA.

**3. Intangible asset lives: the policy range does not cover lives used elsewhere in the notes**
- **Shown:** The Note 2 lives table now says "Other intangible assets | 3 to 10 years" (p.91; it was "3 to 20 years", FY2012 p.81). Note 2 also says "two to fifteen years" (p.93), and Donlen's intangibles carry 16- and 20-year lives (p.99). Not quantified. Evidence level 1, in the audited notes (text-2 table row 4).
- **Reading:** Can't tell from these documents; the earlier reply gave no reading.
- **Next:** the lives actually applied to each intangible class, and amortization by class.

**4. Goodwill: the level at which impairment is tested is described two ways**
- **Shown:**
  - Note 2 says testing is "one level below our operating segments" (p.93; identical in FY2012 p.83).
  - MD&A lists five reporting units (p.46; the same five in FY2012 p.46), and these match the operating segments in Note 11 (p.130–131).
  - No headroom is disclosed; the notes say only "no impairment existed" (p.94).
  - Not quantified. Balances: goodwill $1,347.5m; goodwill plus indefinite-lived intangibles $4,697.6m, 1.7 times equity. Evidence level 1, contradicted by MD&A.
  - Absorbs: text-1 write-up 3 and profile row 4.
- **Reading:** Most likely ordinary loose wording (text-1). The same text in FY2012 weakens text-1's specific explanation that it was left over from the Q3 2013 segment change. Reporting units that had been combined would point elsewhere.
- **Next:** fair-value headroom by reporting unit and for the trade name.

**5. Controls: errors and a missed financing condition in a year controls were judged effective**
- **Shown:**
  - $46.3m of out-of-period errors were found in Q4 2013 (p.83): vendor incentives booked against marketing instead of vehicle cost, plus Brazil allowances.
  - Management and PwC concluded internal control was effective (p.74, p.159). The 2011 revision was 5.8% of that year's pre-tax income and was called not material.
  - A new Oracle general ledger went live in Q3 2013 (p.159; new this year).
  - A requirement under the HVF II fleet notes was "unknowingly not met" at year-end (p.108).
  - Size: 2013 share of the errors $11.6m = 1.7%. Evidence level 2.
  - Absorbs: text-1 profile row 8 and also-noted items; text-2 other change 5.
  - Several items under "Also noted" are much larger, but they are balances or exposures and rank lower.
- **Reading:** Can't tell from these documents (text-1).
- **Next:** how management evaluated these deficiencies, and the revised 2013 quarterly reports.

### Also noted

- **Sobel litigation:** the company calls the outcome immaterial, which text-1 judged to lean aggressive; the new "continue to believe" wording refers to a view not stated in FY2012. Exposure ≥$40.0m = 6.0% (text-1, text-2).
- **Multiemployer pension risk factor** softened while four plans remain "Critical".
- **Cash-flow measure replaced:** "free cash flow" replaced levered cash flow; 2012 is shown as +$155.1m after excluding $129.6m of Dollar Thrifty items.
- **Possible double count:** $21.9m of integration expense equals the $21.9m headquarters relocation booked in restructuring.
- **Public liability reserve:** no claims-development data, so it can't be judged.
- **Receivables:** excluding vehicle-sale receivables, they fell 9.6% against revenue up 19.4%; no factoring is disclosed.
- **Tie-out gaps:** impairment $40.0m vs $44.0m; bad-debt provision $45.9m vs $41.9m.
- **Revised prior-year figures:** 2012 pre-tax income $450.6m → $441.4m; allowance $25.1m → $29.3m.

### Checked and explained
- Receivables vs industry: disclosed vehicle-sale receivables cover 137% of the gap.
- Operating margin vs industry: pre-tax margin is 6.2% after fleet interest.
- Donlen's guaranteed residuals show $0 gain or loss in both years.
- The 9-year life for concession agreements fits the amortization.
- The error components add up ($34.7m prior years + $11.6m in 2013 = $46.3m).
- The restructuring accrual rolls forward to $48.4m.
- Equipment and property lives fit the depreciation charged, roughly.
