**Hertz Global Holdings: FY2013 (to 31 Dec 2013), compared with FY2012**
Documents: the FY2013 and FY2012 annual reports, the Q4 FY2013 and Q4 FY2012 results releases, and the statements spreadsheet. Pre-tax income is $663.1m.

### Items to investigate

**1. Rental-car depreciation: lower rates while resale prices fell**
- **Shown:**
  - Note 8 says the U.S. rate cuts were "indicative of the residual values experienced in the U.S." The same note reports a $48.2m loss on U.S. car sales "due to … declining residual values", against a $100.6m gain in 2012 (FY13 p.126).
  - Year on year, the sentence lost the word "strong" (FY12 p.113).
  - The top of the car holding-period range moved from 28 to 36 months (FY12 p.81 → FY13 p.91), but the stated U.S. average stayed at 18 months (p.11). The report does not say how the average is computed.
  - The depreciation rate fell from 15.3% to 14.3%, and residual values disappeared from the 2013 explanation (p.42).
  - Size: the company's stated rate cuts are $44.2m (6.7%), or $39.6m net (6.0%). The depreciation not charged at the FY2012 rate is $152.1m on the revised basis (22.9%; $174.5m first reported). **Ranks on 22.9%.**
  - **Level 1, audited notes.**
- **Reading:** The two tracks disagree, and so do my two earlier text replies.
  - First text reply: most likely the business weakening (residual values fell faster than rates were adjusted).
  - Second text reply and the numbers track: possibly the reporting stretched.
  - They also tested the MD&A per-vehicle claim differently. The text track found it fits roughly ($218 → $216 per car-month). The numbers track found it can't be tested as stated, and its nearest measure rose 2.9%.
  - With disposal adjustments added back, the rate is flat at 14.5%.
  - What would change the reading: losses that came from the timing of sales, not from residual values, would point to ordinary.
- **Next:** the quarterly split of the $44.2m and the $48.2m, which vehicles the 36-month range covers, and 2014 disposal results.

**2. Adjusted pre-tax income: recurring costs called "one-time"**
- **Shown:**
  - The measure adds back "certain one-time charges" (MD&A p.50), yet restructuring is added back every year: $66.2m, then $49.1m, then $98.8m. Restructuring totals $645.4m since 2007, and more is planned (p.140).
  - The 2012 "Other" line was relabelled "Other unusual/non-recurring", and it carries $50.7m again in 2013.
  - New 2013 exclusions total $171.2m.
  - Size: new exclusions 25.8%; restructuring plus related charges 14.9%; all add-backs 73.9%. **Ranks on 25.8%.**
  - **Level 1, MD&A and adjusted measure.**
- **Reading:** Possibly the reporting stretched; both text replies read it this way. The reading would become ordinary if the integration and relocation costs stop in 2014.
- **Next:** the 2014 reconciliations.

**3. Intangible-asset lives: the stated range does not cover the actual lives**
- **Shown:** Note 2 says lives "range from two to fifteen years" (p.93). Donlen intangibles are amortized over 16 and 20 years (p.99). The policy table narrowed from "3 to 20" to "3 to 10" years (p.91) while those lives stayed the same.
  - Size: 0.06% (first reply) or under 0.1% (second reply).
  - **Level 1, audited notes.** It is far smaller than items 4 to 5 and the Q4 margin item below.
- **Reading:** Most likely ordinary: the policy text was not updated. It would read differently if other acquired intangibles also carry lives beyond the range.
- **Next:** a schedule of remaining lives by class of intangible.

**4. Out-of-period errors, centred on fleet cost and the doubtful-account allowance**
- **Shown:**
  - $46.3m of errors were found in Q4 2013 (p.83). They include vendor incentives booked against marketing expense instead of fleet cost, and Brazil allowance charges.
  - Prior years were revised: 2012 pre-tax income went from $450.6m to $441.4m, and the 2012 allowance from $25.1m to $29.3m.
  - The 2013 provision is $45.9m in the cash flow (p.80) but $41.9m in Schedule II (p.158), a gap of 0.6%.
  - The allowance rose from 1.31% to 2.00% of gross receivables.
  - The 2012 restructuring roll-forward was recast (p.141).
  - A new Oracle ledger went live in Q3 2013, and no material weakness is reported (p.159).
  - Size: $11.6m booked in 2013 (1.7%). **Level 2.**
- **Reading:** No earlier reply chose a reading, so it can't be read from these documents. It would read as ordinary if the corrections are confined to the items named.
- **Next:** the auditor's communications on the corrections, and the 2014 10-Qs for further revisions.

**5. Payables: twice the industry level, and falling**
- **Shown:**
  - Payables were 9.0% of sales against 4.5% for the industry; the excess is $483.2m (balance, 72.9%).
  - Days payable fell from 52.9 to 42.7, worth $232.8m (balance, 35.1%). Dollar Thrifty explains only 22% of the fall.
  - The balance fell $35.3m, but the cash flow shows payables adding $23.2m. The $58.5m difference is unreconciled.
  - The company gives no explanation. **Level 2, balance only.**
- **Reading:** Can't tell from these documents (numbers track). A split showing suppliers tightening terms would point to the business weakening; payables moved into accrued liabilities would point to the reporting stretched.
- **Next:** a breakdown of payables into fleet, capex and trade.

### Also noted
- **Q4 gross margin** fell from 22.1% to 18.5% (13.6%, level 3). The company's explanation covers only about 45%. The reading is most likely the business weakening. This item is larger than items 3 to 5.
- **Sobel case:** a judgment that "could potentially exceed $40.0 million" (exposure, 6.0%; p.137), with no accrual stated.
- **New securities class action** (p.138).
- **Goodwill test:** no headroom is disclosed (p.94).
- **Simply Wheelz:** a $44.0m charge, with about 9,000 vehicles still held (p.24).
- **Public liability reserve:** "relatively minor adjustments" can't be tested (p.45).

### Checked and explained
- **Receivables above the industry level:** amounts owed on fleet sales account for the difference. Without them, receivables are 10.7% of sales against 11.5% for the industry.
- **International "slight strengthening" of residual values:** fits once both years are compared. Disposal losses narrowed from $17.3m to $15.2m.
- **Lease tables:** tie to $2,727.7m.
- **Concession amortization:** fits the stated nine-year life.

### Not examined
Payables by type, quarterly depreciation and disposal results, and goodwill headroom were not available. Related parties are covered in the proxy, which is not attached.

Items to investigate, not conclusions. The verdicts are the analyst's.
