**Flags**

1. **Payables vs industry:** in FY2013, payables were 9.0% of sales against the industry's 4.5%, about twice the industry level. The cash flow statement shows $289.1m of fleet purchases unpaid at year-end, held in payables and accrued liabilities.
   Size: $483.2m (payables less payables at 4.5% of sales), 72.9% of pre-tax income. Industry figure is for one year only; payables as a share of sales fell in 2013.
2. **Receivables vs industry:** in FY2013, receivables were 14.0% of sales against 11.5% (42 days at the industry share vs 51.3 actual). Receivables include $357.3m still owed on fleet vehicles the company sold.
   Size: $273.8m (receivables less receivables at 11.5% of sales), 41.3% of pre-tax income. Receivables as a share of sales fell in 2013, after rising in 2011 and 2012.
3. **Payables fell behind costs:** days payable fell from 52.7 to 42.7 in FY2013, a gap of −22.3 points against cost growth. In the same quarter they fell from 50.8 to 42.8. Dollar Thrifty was acquired on 19 Nov 2012, so its payables were in the year-end 2012 balance with only about 43 days of its costs.
   Size: $226.3m (payables at FY2012 days payable less actual), 34.1% of pre-tax income. Annual decline in 1 year; Q4 same-quarter decline in 2 consecutive comparisons.
4. **Depreciation rate fell:** the rental-equipment rate went from 16.2% (FY2011) to 15.3% (FY2012) to 14.3% (FY2013). The annual reports disclose rate changes on U.S. cars that cut depreciation by $139.4m in 2012 and $44.2m in 2013.
   Size: $174.5m (FY2013 charge not taken at the FY2012 rate), 26.3% of pre-tax income. 2 consecutive years.
5. **Q4 gross margin fell:** 22.1% to 18.5%, Q4 2012 to Q4 2013. Note 8 shows 2013 net losses on selling U.S. cars ($48.2m), against gains of $100.6m in 2012.
   Size: $90.2m (3.5-point fall × Q4 2013 revenue of $2,556.3m), 13.6% of pre-tax income. 1 comparison: the Q4 2012 margin had risen from 21.3% in Q4 2011.
