**Hertz Global Holdings, Inc.: FY2013 (year to 31 Dec 2013), compared with FY2012**
Sources: 10-Ks for FY2012 and FY2013, Q4 results releases for both years, statements.xlsx, and the two numbers-track replies. PTI is FY2013 pre-tax income of $663.1m.

### Items to investigate

**1. Car depreciation: lower rates, longer holding periods, and a claim the report itself contradicts**
- **Shown:** Note 8 says the US rate cuts were "indicative of the residual values experienced in the U.S." in 2013. The same note blames 2013 disposal losses on "declining residual values" (p126); the MD&A says "deterioration" (p54).
  - US disposals swung from a $100.6m gain to a $48.2m loss.
  - The car holding period range was lengthened from 4–28 to 4–36 months (FY2012 p81 → FY2013 p91), but is not called a change in estimate. A new reason was added, "optimization of fleet holding periods" (p126). Last year's word "strong" was dropped (FY2012 p113).
  - The depreciation rate on rental equipment fell from 16.2% (2011) to 15.3% to 14.2%, and the non-program share of the US fleet fell from 95% to 91%. That mix shift should raise depreciation, not lower it.
  - Size (income effects): $174.5m charge not taken at the 2012 rate as first reported, 26.3% of PTI ($152.1m revised, 22.9%), from the numbers track. The company's own stated effect is $44.2m, 6.7% (net of other segments, $39.6m, 6.0%).
  - Evidence: level 1.
- **Reading:** The replies don't fully agree.
  - Possibly stretched reporting: both tracks' main write-ups read it this way, because residual assumptions were raised in the year cars sold at a loss.
  - Most likely the business weakening, with rates lagging used-car prices: one text-track write-up on the dropped word "strong."
  - Ordinary: if the losses came from cars bought under the old rates, or Dollar Thrifty's longer holding periods justify the new rates.
- **Next:** Quarterly rate changes and disposal results for 2013 by segment, and whether rates were raised in 2014.

**2. Adjusted pre-tax income: recurring costs treated as "one-time," and new add-backs**
- **Shown:** The measure excludes "certain one-time charges" (p50), yet restructuring has been charged every year: $645.4m over 2007–2013 (p140).
  - Five 2013 add-back categories weren't used in 2012: integration, relocation, the Simply Wheelz impairment, debt premiums and the convertible-note conversion loss.
  - "Non-cash debt charges" was renamed "debt-related charges" (FY2012 p50 → FY2013 p50).
  - $65.0m of Advantage sublease revenue stays in the measure, while the $44.0m impairment on the same counterparty is removed (p50–51).
  - Size (income effects): new categories $171.2m, 25.8% of PTI; restructuring and related $98.8m, 14.9%. All add-backs total $490.1m, 73.9% of PTI (102% in 2012).
  - Evidence: level 1.
- **Reading:** Most likely stretched reporting: a cost charged for seven years running is ongoing. It would read as one-off only if the 2013 charges were mostly the HQ move and the Dollar Thrifty integration.
- **Next:** 2014 filings, to see whether restructuring continues and is still added back.

**3. Accounts payable fell while costs rose; the company's explanation isn't supported**
- **Shown:** Days payable fell from 52.7 to 42.7 as payables dropped 3.1% while cost of sales rose 19.2%. The company says operating cash flow rose partly through "timing of our payments" (p64), but payables and accruals explain only 4.5% of the $879.9m rise.
  - The cash flow line (+$23.2m) points the opposite way from the balance sheet (−$35.3m).
  - An Oracle accounts payable system went live in Q3 2013 (p159).
  - Size: a balance of $226.3m, 34.1% of PTI; not an income effect.
  - Evidence: level 1.
- **Reading:** Can't tell from these documents. It could be faster payment, which is ordinary, or a reclassification after the system change.
- **Next:** A breakdown of payables and accrued liabilities, and the Q3 2013 10-Q.

**4. Self-insured liability reserve: the stated method and the numbers diverge**
- **Shown:** The reserve is "based on rental volume" in both years (p45). Rental days rose 19.8%, but the reserve rose only 4.7%, and the reserve per 1,000 days fell below its 2011 level.
  - The claim of "relatively minor adjustments" and the attribution of the increase to Dollar Thrifty (p138) can't be tested: there is no roll-forward.
  - Size: $17.7m–$50.2m, 2.7%–7.6% of PTI (rough income effect). Dollar Thrifty's part-year in 2012 distorts the comparison. The $347.7m reserve is a balance.
  - Evidence: level 2.
- **Reading:** Can't tell: not enough disclosed.
- **Next:** An actuarial reserve roll-forward and claims development history.

**5. Prior-year errors with an unqualified controls opinion**
- **Shown:** Q4 2013 found errors reaching back to 2010, in vendor incentives and Brazil allowances (p83). Internal control is still reported effective, with no deficiency discussed (p159).
  - The error total differs between documents: $46.3m in the 10-K versus $47.9m in the release.
  - The new securities suit's class period, Feb–Nov 2013 (p138), overlaps the revised quarters.
  - Size: $11.6m of the errors fall in 2013, 1.7% of PTI (income effect).
  - Evidence: level 2.
- **Reading:** Can't tell.
- **Next:** The auditor's communications on these errors and the 2014 controls assessment.
- **Larger item one level down:** the Q4 gross margin fell from 22.1% to 18.5%. That is $90.2m, 13.6% of PTI, much larger than this item, but evidence is only level 3. It most likely reflects a weakening business: lower US pricing and excess fleet.

### Also noted
- **Sobel lawsuit:** a judgment "could potentially exceed $40.0 million" (an exposure, 6.0% of PTI), but the amount accrued is not stated, and a new "will not be material" assurance appeared (p137).
- **Goodwill:** no headroom disclosed, and reporting units are described two ways (p46, p93).
- **Bad-debt allowance:** coverage of write-offs fell from 1.00× to 0.76× (rough, 1.4% of PTI), and the 2012 allowance was restated from $25.1m to $29.3m.
- **Intangible lives:** three conflicting ranges in the FY2013 report (p91, p93, p99).
- **Tax reserves** "released" with no amount given (p40).
- **2012 property disposal gains** restated from $6.3m to $8.3m without explanation.
- **Pricing metric** redefined to include fees (FY2012 p49 → FY2013 p49).

### Checked and explained
- **Receivables decline:** vehicle-sale receivables account for 71% of the fall; most likely ordinary.
- **Free cash flow below net income:** most likely ordinary fleet growth after the acquisition.
- **US per-car depreciation fell:** the claim holds.
- **Tie-outs:** the two 2012 rate-change figures ($130.6m vs $139.4m) differ only because one nets international. The lease tables, restructuring totals, valuation allowance, pension expense and error sums all tie out.

### Not examined
The documents don't include:
- a roll-forward of the self-insured reserve;
- goodwill headroom;
- quarterly depreciation rate changes;
- a payables breakdown;
- a 2009 balance sheet;
- critical audit matters (none in either report).

Items to investigate, not conclusions. The verdicts are the analyst's.
