## 1. Write-ups

**Depreciation: longer car holding periods and a new reason for lower depreciation**
- **Before:** Revenue earning equipment "Useful lives… Cars | 4 to 28 months" (FY2012 AR, Note 2, p81). The rate cuts got no business reason; the note only says they were "indicative of the strong residual values experienced" (p113).
- **After:** "Holding periods… Cars | 4 to 36 months" (FY2013 AR, Note 2, p91). A reason is now given: "The favorable adjustments reflect… the optimization of fleet holding periods related to the integration of Dollar Thrifty" (Note 8, p126).
- **Why it matters:** The top of the range rose by 8 months, and the company now ties the $44.2m cut in US depreciation to holding cars longer. The figures move the same way:
  - car depreciation fell from 16.9% to 15.4% of average gross car cost;
  - the fleet's average age in depreciation-months rose from 12.0 to 15.4. This is rough: it treats all depreciation outside equipment rental as cars and includes Donlen.

  The longer period is not identified as a change in estimate. Size: 6.7% of PTI, the stated US rate effect; the share due to longer holding periods is not separated.
- **Reading:** Possibly stretched reporting. Holding periods were lengthened in the same year that US disposals swung from a $100.6m gain to a $48.2m loss, so book values were already above resale values. If the 36-month cars are mainly Dollar Thrifty's existing fleet practice rather than longer lives for Hertz cars, the reading would be ordinary.

**Depreciation: "strong" dropped while resale results turned to losses**
- **Before:** "The cumulative effect of the reduction in rates was indicative of the **strong** residual values experienced in the U.S." (FY2012 AR, Note 8, p113).
- **After:** "…was **also** indicative of the residual values experienced in the U.S." (FY2013 AR, Note 8, p126). Same note, new sentence: "declining residual values from falling demand for used vehicles."
- **Why it matters:** The adjective went, but the claim stayed, even though car disposals moved from an $83.2m net gain in 2012 (FY2012 basis) to a $63.4m net loss in 2013 (US $48.2m, international $15.2m). My last reply showed the claim fails for 2013; the comparison shows the company softened the wording instead of withdrawing it. Size: 6.7% of PTI (the rate cuts the sentence defends).
- **Reading:** Most likely the business weakening, with depreciation rates lagging used-car prices. Rate increases or a reversed sentence in 2014 filings would confirm that; continued rate cuts would point to stretched reporting.

**Legal: an amount on Sobel, a materiality assurance, and a securities suit**
- **Before:** No amount for Sobel. The case is "in the discovery stages"; "the parties will again be engaging in mediation" (FY2012 AR, Note 12, p122).
- **After:** "A judgment - which could potentially exceed $40.0 million"; "We continue to believe the outcome of this case will not be material" (FY2013 AR, Note 13, p137). New: "Pedro Ramirez, Jr. v. Hertz Global Holdings," alleging misstatements "from February 25, 2013 through November 4, 2013" (p138).
- **Why it matters:** Two things matter here:
  - The new amount is 6.0% of PTI, but the amount accrued is still not given.
  - The "continue to believe" assurance was not in last year's Sobel text.
  - The securities suit's class period covers the same quarters the company later revised for out-of-period errors (p83–87).

  Size: exposure of more than $40m (6.0% of PTI); the securities suit is not quantified.
- **Reading:** Can't tell. The report does not say whether any reserve covers Sobel. Disclosure of an accrued amount or a settlement figure would settle it.

## 2. Other changes
- **Adjusted pre-tax income definition:** "non-cash debt charges" (FY2012 AR p50; FY2012 ER p19) became "debt-related charges" (FY2013 AR p50; FY2013 ER p3).
  - Add-back categories not used in 2012 total $171.2m, 25.8% of PTI: integration $40.0m, relocation $7.8m, Simply Wheelz impairment $44.0m, debt premiums $28.7m, convertible-note conversion loss $50.7m.
  - Restructuring is still treated as "one-time."
- **Pricing metric redefined:** last year's "rental rate revenue per transaction day" excluded fees; 2012 US was $39.07 (FY2012 AR p49). This year's "Total RPD" includes them; 2012 US is restated to $46.33 (FY2013 AR p49). The ER also replaced "Corporate cash flow" (FY2012 ER p22) with "Free cash flow" (FY2013 ER p3).
- **Prior-year figures restated:**
  - 2012 pre-tax income went from $450.6m to $441.4m; revenue earning equipment depreciation from $2,165.2m to $2,145.9m.
  - The 2012 bad-debt allowance went from $25.1m to $29.3m (Sch. II: FY2012 p140, FY2013 p158).
  - 2012 gains on property disposals went from $6.3m to $8.3m with no explanation (FY2012 p81; FY2013 p91).
- **Controls:** the Dollar Thrifty exclusion was dropped (FY2012 p71, p141). New text says "the Company implemented Oracle general ledger…" in Q3 2013 (FY2013 p159). Internal control is still reported effective, even though errors were found in Q4.
- **Critical estimates:** added "industry residual value guidebooks" and "Depreciation rates are adjusted prospectively through the remaining expected life"; dropped "and assessing depreciation rates" (FY2012 p44; FY2013 p45).
