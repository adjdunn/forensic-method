Cost of sales is direct operating costs plus depreciation of revenue earning equipment and lease charges. Some measures use substitutes:
- **Q4 figures:** Q4 2013 and Q4 2012 come from the FY2013 results release because the spreadsheet's Q4 2013 column is marked mixed basis. That release shows Q4 2012 as revised. Q4 balances are as first reported.
- **Capex:** rental equipment and property purchases, net of disposal proceeds.
- **Depreciation:** the Note 8 "Depreciation of revenue earning equipment" line.
- **M-score:** current assets are cash, restricted cash, receivables, inventories and prepaids, because the balance sheet doesn't split current from non-current. Leverage uses total liabilities.
- **Industry:** the Transportation row; the Industry sheet has no rental row.
