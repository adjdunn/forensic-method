**Flags (largest first; size is a share of pre-tax income for the year to February 2026, $18,602m)**

1. **Free cash flow fell below net income.** It went from 1.19× net income (FY Jan-24) to 1.10× and then 0.89× (FY Feb-26). The Q4 comparison shows the same drop, 1.19× to 0.89×. The statements show nothing that explains it.
   Size: net income less free cash flow, $1,510m, or **8.1%**. It has fallen for 2 years in a row.
2. **Operating margin fell.** It went from 15.3% to 14.2%, 13.5% and then 12.7% over FY Jan-23 to FY Feb-26. Q4 fell from 11.9% to 11.3% and then 10.1%. A possible cause: amortization of intangibles from the SRS and GMS purchases rose from $425m to $607m.
   Size: the 0.81-point fall × FY Feb-26 sales, $1,334m, or **7.2%**. It has fallen for 3 years and in 2 Q4 comparisons.
3. **Inventory grew faster than cost of sales.** Days inventory went from 75.1 to 81.9 and then 85.6 (FY Jan-24 to FY Feb-26). Q4 went from 86.2 to 91.3, a gap of 13.6 points over cost-of-sales growth. A possible cause: the GMS acquisition ($5,410m, September 2025).
   Size: the balance less what it would be at the prior year's days, $1,102m, or **5.9%** (the Q4 equivalent is $1,450m, or 7.8%). It has risen for 2 years and in 2 Q4 comparisons.
4. **Payables fell behind cost of sales.** Days payable went from 41.7 to 38.1 in the year to February 2026, and from 43.9 to 40.6 in Q4. The level, 7.0% of sales, is also below the industry's 9.4%, about $3,989m less than it would be at the industry share.
   Size: what payables would be at the prior year's days less the balance, $1,090m, or **5.9%**. This is the first year of decline, and the first Q4 comparison.
5. **Receivables grew faster than sales.** Days sales outstanding went from 7.9 to 11.4 and then 12.4. Q4 went from 8.7 to 12.1 and then 13.3. The level, 3.4% of sales, is above the industry's 2.8%. Customer receivables went from $924m to $1,896m and then $2,588m. A possible cause: the SRS (FY Feb-25) and GMS (FY Feb-26) acquisitions.
   Size: the balance less what it would be at the prior year's days, $438m, or **2.4%** (the excess over the industry share is $986m, or 5.3%). It has risen for 3 years and in 2 Q4 comparisons.

The M-score stays below −1.78 in every year. Its high point (−2.12, FY Feb-25) comes from a receivables index of 1.41 and an asset-quality index of 1.46, the year of the SRS purchase.
