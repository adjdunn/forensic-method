## Consistency check: Hertz Global Holdings, FY2012 to FY2013

- **Fleet depreciation rate** = Note 8 depreciation (before disposal adjustments) ÷ the average of five quarter-end gross fleet balances.
- **U.S. per-car figures** = segment fleet depreciation ÷ average company-operated cars (ER-Q4-FY2013 Tables 4 and 7).

| Check | Sentence (quoted, page) | Number | Result | Innocent reading | Concerning reading |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Impairment | "…concluded that there was no impairment related to our goodwill and our other indefinite lived intangible assets." (AR-FY2013 critical estimates, p. 46) | International pre-tax $45.3m to $41.8m. Q4 loss -$26.6m to -$34.5m. No multi-year decline. | **Can't test.** Reporting-unit values are not disclosed. | – | – |
| 2. Holding periods | "…our approximate average holding period for a rental car was eighteen months in the United States…" (AR-FY2013 Business, p. 11; same in FY2012) vs "…longer holding periods and the impact of recalls." (MD&A, p. 53) | Car range 4-28 to 4-36 months. Fleet depreciation rate **15.2% / 15.0% / 13.6%** (2011-13). | **Inconsistent.** Longer holding is cited as a cost driver, but the average is unchanged while the range widens and the rate falls. | The average rounds to 18 months, and the blended rate moves with fleet mix. | Holding periods were lengthened in the estimate to lower depreciation, unseen in the stated average. |
| 3a. Per-car cost | "…our U.S. monthly per vehicle depreciation costs decreased as compared to the prior year period…" (MD&A, p. 42) | **$219 to $226 per car per month (+2.9%)**; before disposal losses, -10.5%. Guidance was "no less than 4% to 5%" lower (ER-Q4-FY2012, p. 5). | **Inconsistent** once disposal losses are included. | The metric excludes disposal results. | The metric omits the $48.2m of losses that show past depreciation was too low. |
| 3b. Rate cuts vs losses | "These depreciation rate changes in our U.S. car rental operations … resulted in net decreases of $44.2 million…" and "…also indicative of the residual values experienced in the U.S." (Note 8, p. 126) | Same note: U.S. disposals went from a $100.6m gain to a **$48.2m loss**, from "declining residual values". MD&A p. 54: "a deterioration in the used vehicle residual values". | **Inconsistent.** Both halves are in the same note. | The cuts are prospective, for newer cars sold at retail; the losses are on older cars. | Book values exceed market, yet depreciation was lowered, deferring the shortfall. |
| 3c. Public-liability reserve | "The increase in public liability and property damage reserves was primarily related to Dollar Thrifty." (Legal, p. 138) | +$15.5m, although Dollar Thrifty was already in the 2012 balance. Cash-flow line -$3.7m. | **Can't test.** No rollforward. | Acquisition-accounting true-up. | Adverse claims development attributed to the acquisition. |
| 3d. Error total | "…out of period errors totaling $46.3 million…" (Note 2, p. 83) | The release says "$47.9 million" (ER-Q4-FY2013, Table 1 note). | **Inconsistent**, by $1.6m. | The release was drafted early. | Scope of the errors changed after the release. |
| 4. "Non-recurring" | "…certain one-time charges and nonoperational items." (MD&A, p. 50) | Restructuring 56.4 / 38.0 / 77.0, and $645.4m since 2007. Acquisition costs every year. "Other unusual/non-recurring" 44.0 / 50.7. | **Inconsistent.** | Distinct events each year. | Recurring costs are excluded. |
| 5. Reserve releases | "Our actual results as compared to our estimates have historically resulted in relatively minor adjustments to our recorded liability." (critical estimates, p. 45) | No release is described. Allowance: 2013 provision $41.9m exceeds write-offs of $40.3m. | **Can't test** public liability; allowance **consistent**. | – | – |
| 6a. Prior-year restructuring | FY2012 Note 13 table (p. 126) vs FY2013 reprint (Note 14, p. 141) | 2012 cash payments $(26.8)m reprinted as **$(36.8)m**; "Other" $(11.8)m reprinted as $(1.8)m; footnote reason changed. | **Inconsistent**, unexplained. | Lease settlements reclassified. | Restructuring cash flows misstated. |
| 6b. Other 2012 reprints | Note 8, Schedule II, advertising, adjusted pre-tax income | -19.3; +4.2; +25.9 and +22.4; -9.2 | **Consistent.** Each ties to the Note 2 corrections. | – | – |

**Summary.** Inconsistencies:
- U.S. rate cuts justified by residual values, against losses from declining residuals in the same note;
- "per-vehicle depreciation decreased", which holds only before losses;
- "longer holding periods" against an unchanged 18-month average;
- "one-time" items that recur;
- an unexplained $10.0m reprint of 2012 restructuring payments;
- an error total that differs from the release.

Minor: intangible lives (3-10 years in the table vs "two to fifteen years" in the text).
