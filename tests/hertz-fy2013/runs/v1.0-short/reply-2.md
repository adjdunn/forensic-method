No year exceeds -1.78. The main drivers are:
- **TATA**, worth -0.5 to -0.6 every year;
- **SGI**, worth up to +0.17;
- **DSRI** in 2013, worth -0.30.

TATA is always strongly negative because operating cash flow adds back about $2.4bn of fleet depreciation, while fleet purchases sit in investing. So the screen cannot see Hertz's biggest estimate. Read the fleet depreciation and disposal notes instead.
