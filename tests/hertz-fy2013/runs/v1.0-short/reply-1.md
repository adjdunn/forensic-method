## Working capital: Hertz Global Holdings (USD m)

- **Basis.** Quarters are as first reported. The FY2013 10-K revised 2012, so FY2012 is shown both ways. Q4 2013 uses the release's revised revenue and costs, because the spreadsheet's derived Q4 2013 mixes bases.
- **Cost line.** There is no cost of sales, so I use "Direct operating" (it excludes fleet depreciation).
- **Customer receivables.** Receivables less vehicle and property sale proceeds still due (cash-flow supplemental line).
